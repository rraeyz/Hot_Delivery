## Kısa açıklama (EN)
Stack the orders. Keep the soup in the bowl. Don't tip over. A physics comedy roguelite about delivering a wobbling tower of food on a terrible scooter — solo, as driver + passenger, or split-screen.

## Uzun açıklama (EN)
**Soup is not meant to travel at 60 km/h. Deliver it anyway.**

Every order goes on the back of your scooter — on top of the last one. Pizza boxes slide, burgers lean, soup sloshes, ice cream melts, and the wedding cake… the wedding cake is a problem. Brake too hard and the tower goes. Take a corner too fast and the soup goes. Hit a speed bump and everything goes.

- **The food tower:** carry more orders for bigger combos — if you can keep them stacked
- **Balcony throws:** skip the doorbell, launch the top order onto the balcony, double the tip
- **Customer reviews** you'll want to screenshot ("The soup arrived. The bowl didn't.")
- **Pay the rent or get fired:** a roguelite run of shifts, 14 weird upgrades with side effects, daily city chaos (rain, loose dogs, hungry pigeons, speed bump day…)
- **Disaster replay** of your worst crash every shift
- **Daily Run:** same city, same chaos for everyone today
- **Couch co-op:** one drives, one balances the tower — or two scooters in split screen. Works online with Steam Remote Play Together.
